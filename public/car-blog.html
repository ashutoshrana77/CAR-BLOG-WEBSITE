India's 10 Most Sold Cars Till Now

From the iconic Maruti 800 to the latest SUVs, these cars shaped how India moves.

Over the decades, a few models have dominated Indian roads, combining affordability, reliability, and mass appeal. This blog highlights 10 of the most sold and influential cars in India, along with their typical price, average life, and why they became so popular.

Maruti 800

#maruti-800
Top 10 Indian Automotive Icons • Rank #1
Approx. Cumulative Sales
Over 27 lakh units sold in India before discontinuation. [81]
Price (Last Known)
Around ₹2.4–3.0 lakh (ex-showroom, before discontinuation).
Average Life
Typically 12–15 years or 1.5–2.0 lakh km with regular maintenance.
Context & History

The Maruti 800 is often called the car that motorised India's middle class. Launched in 1983, it became the default choice for families, taxi operators, and first-time car buyers.

Its small size, low running cost, and easy maintenance made it an icon on Indian roads for over three decades.

Though discontinued in 2014 due to stricter safety and emission norms, the Maruti 800 remains a cultural symbol of affordable mobility.

Its compact dimensions make it ideal for crowded Indian streets, while its strong resale value keeps it popular in the used-car market.
